Full-Time · Actively Recruiting

Postdoctoral Fellows

Independent and collaborative research in translational oncology, phosphoproteomics, structural biology, or computational/spatial cancer biology.

  • Ph.D. in Molecular Biology, Oncology, Bioinformatics, or Computer Science / Medical Imaging.
  • Strong publication record in peer-reviewed international journals.
  • Lead research in WBP2 signaling, biomarker discovery, or spatial tumor microenvironment analysis.
  • Prepare manuscripts, grants, and co-mentor graduate students.
  • Competitive Ningbo University Postdoctoral salary package, research support, and career mentorship.

Research Focus Areas

Molecular & Translational Oncology

Dissecting the molecular mechanisms of the WBP2 oncogene and its regulation of Hippo, Wnt, PI3K/AKT, and AMPK signaling pathways in human cancers.

Biomarker Discovery & Clinical Validation

Applying quantitative phosphoproteomics and secretomics to discover blood-based and tissue biomarkers for early cancer detection and in vitro diagnostics (IVD).

Computational & Spatial Cancer Biology

Using deep learning, computational histopathology, and multimodal imaging to map the spatial architecture of the tumor microenvironment.

Therapeutic Target Development

Integrating structural biology, antibody engineering, and drug discovery to develop targeted therapies that overcome clinical drug resistance.
