Core Laboratory Vision

“Staying true to the core values of medicine and research (people-centric) and advancing a Healthy Ningbo (community & society-centric).”

Prof. Lim Yoon Pin (林允斌) is an internationally recognized expert in oncology with over 20 years of experience in molecular, translational, and clinical cancer research. Recruited as a Yangtze River Scholar—the highest academic honor awarded by China's Ministry of Education—his pioneer work focuses on phosphoproteomics, the WBP2 oncogene, and precision cancer diagnostics.

Key Research Highlights

Phosphoproteomics

Pioneered advanced phosphoproteomics methodologies to discover novel cancer biomarkers and therapeutic targets.

WBP2 Oncogene Discovery

Identified and validated WBP2 as an authentic novel oncogene, establishing global leadership in WBP2-driven oncogenesis (authoring ~33% of global publications in this field).

Translational & Precision Oncology

Successfully translated WBP2 scientific discoveries into in vitro diagnostics (IVD) for early cancer detection, prognosis, and precision medicine.
