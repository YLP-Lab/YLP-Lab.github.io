**Wang Xiaofang**, graduated from Liaoning University of Traditional Chinese Medicine, currently works at the Health Science Center / School of Medicine of Ningbo University as an Assistant Researcher. Her research focuses on the mechanisms of small-molecule drugs in anti-tumor effects. She has led one project under the Natural Science Foundation of Ningbo City and one project under the Science and Technology Medicine Project of Ningbo City, and participated in one project under the Natural Science Foundation of Zhejiang Province.
