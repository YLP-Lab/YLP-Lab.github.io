**Dr. Muhammad Wajeeh Us Sima Rathore** is a Computer Science Scholar and AI Researcher specializing in Deep Learning and Medical Image Analysis. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Chongqing University and is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Ningbo University, where his research focuses on the **Spatial Mapping of the Tumor Microenvironment** in breast cancer. With over 5 years of research experience, Dr. Wajeeh advances AI for complex medical applications, including computational histopathology, stain generalization, and multimodal imaging (MRI, X-ray, and HIP-CT). His interdisciplinary work also encompasses privacy-preserving healthcare AI and intelligent security for Medical IoT (IoMT) systems. Dr. Wajeeh has authored 11+ peer-reviewed journal papers in high-impact venues (_Journal of Big Data_, _IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics_, _BioData Mining_, _Image and Vision Computing_, _Internet of Things_, _Frontiers in Medicine_), alongside conference papers and a book chapter on retinal microvascular analysis for diabetic retinopathy. A former University Lecturer and an active reviewer for prestigious international journals, he was honored with the Chongqing University Academic Research Innovation Award (2025).
