**Dr. Tianyu Ma** is a Postdoctoral Researcher specializing in Epigenetics and Cancer Development, holding a Ph.D. from Peking Union Medical College. His research focuses on the molecular mechanisms driving tumor development, with a specific emphasis on epigenetic regulators, chromatin modifier complexes (such as CRL4B), and their role in breast tumorigenesis and environmental health. Dr. Ma's scholarly contributions include high-impact peer-reviewed publications in journals such as *Cell Death & Differentiation*, *Advanced Science*, and *Frontiers of Medicine*. In addition to his research achievements, he was recognized with the National Voluntary Blood Donation Contribution Award (Bronze Award, 2020–2021) and consecutive Peking Union Medical College Academic Scholarships.
