**Prof. Lim Yoon Pin (林允斌)** is an internationally recognized expert in oncology with over 20 years of experience in molecular, translational, and clinical cancer research. Recruited as a **Yangtze River Scholar**—the highest academic honor awarded by China's Ministry of Education—and appointed **Bao Yu Gan (包玉刚) Distinguished Professor** at the Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, Health Science Center, Ningbo University, his pioneer work focuses on **phosphoproteomics**, the **WBP2 oncogene**, and **precision cancer diagnostics**.

Previously, Prof. Lim served as Director & PI of the Molecular & Translational Cancer Research Centre in Ningbo and for 15 years on the faculty at the School of Medicine, National University of Singapore (NUS), where he also directed the Mass Spectrometry Facility, after completing postdoctoral fellowships at the **Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, Harvard Medical School** and the **National Cancer Centre Singapore**. He has secured **>\$8 Million USD** as PI and **>\$22 Million USD** as Co-I, holds **>10 international patents** (with 4 patented technologies out-licensed), mentored **>100 researchers**, and authored **~70 peer-reviewed papers** in top-tier journals including _EMBO Journal_, _Journal of Clinical Investigation_, _Cancer Research_, _Clinical Cancer Research_, _ACS Nano_, and _Oncogene_.
