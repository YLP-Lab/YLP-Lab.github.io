Dr. Muhammad Wajeeh Us Sima is a Computer Science Scholar and AI Researcher specializing in Deep Learning and Medical Image Analysis. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Chongqing University and is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Ningbo University, where his research focuses on the Spatial Mapping of the Tumor Microenvironment in breast cancer, alongside over 5 years of research experience advancing AI for complex medical applications such as computational histopathology, stain generalization, and multi-modal imaging (MRI, X-ray, and HIP-CT), privacy-preserving healthcare AI, and intelligent security for Medical IoT (IoMT) systems, having authored 11+ peer-reviewed journal papers in high-impact venues (Journal of Big Data, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, BioData Mining, Image and Vision Computing, Internet of Things, Frontiers in Medicine), conference papers, and a book chapter on retinal microvascular analysis for diabetic retinopathy as a former University Lecturer and active journal reviewer who was honored with the Chongqing University Academic Research Innovation Award (2025).
