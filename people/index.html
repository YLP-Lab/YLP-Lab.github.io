Members of the Cancer Precision Medicine & Translational Oncology Laboratory (YLP Lab)

Prof. Lim Yoon Pin (林允斌)
Principal Investigator
Health Science Center, Ningbo University

Prof. Lim Yoon Pin (林允斌) is an internationally recognized expert in oncology with over 20 years of experience in molecular, translational, and clinical cancer research. Recruited as a Yangtze River Scholar—the highest academic honor awarded by China’s Ministry of Education—and appointed Bao Yu Gan (包玉刚) Distinguished Professor at the Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, Health Science Center, Ningbo University, his pioneer work focuses on phosphoproteomics, the WBP2 oncogene, and precision cancer diagnostics.

Previously, Prof. Lim served as Director & PI of the Molecular & Translational Cancer Research Centre in Ningbo and for 15 years on the faculty at the School of Medicine, National University of Singapore (NUS), where he also directed the Mass Spectrometry Facility, after completing postdoctoral fellowships at the Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, Harvard Medical School and the National Cancer Centre Singapore. He has secured >$8 Million USD as PI and >$22 Million USD as Co-I, holds >10 international patents (with 4 patented technologies out-licensed), mentored >100 researchers, and authored ~70 peer-reviewed papers in top-tier journals including EMBO Journal, Journal of Clinical Investigation, Cancer Research, Clinical Cancer Research, ACS Nano, and Oncogene.

Dr. Amir Sabbaghian
Postdoctoral Researcher
Ningbo University

Dr. Amir Sabbaghian is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Ningbo University specializing in Molecular Medicine and Translational Cancer Research. With a strong background in cross-institutional research—including a prior Postdoctoral Fellowship at Zhejiang University and research roles at Golestan University of Medical Sciences—his work bridges oncology, functional genomics, and biomarker discovery. Dr. Sabbaghian’s primary research investigates critical drivers of oncogenesis and therapy response, particularly in HER2-positive breast cancer, non-coding RNA networks, and oncogenic signaling axes such as WBP2 and WFIKKN2. His broader expertise includes in silico drug repurposing, pan-cancer transcriptomics, and circulating blood-based biomarker signatures for early detection and disease monitoring. To date, he has authored 9 peer-reviewed publications across top-tier biomedical journals—including Cells, Biomarker Research, and the International Journal of Cancer—and presented at international scientific conferences.

Dr. Tianyu Ma
Postdoctoral Researcher
Ningbo University

Dr. Tianyu Ma is a Postdoctoral Researcher specializing in Epigenetics and Cancer Development, holding a Ph.D. from Peking Union Medical College. His research focuses on the molecular mechanisms driving tumor development, with a specific emphasis on epigenetic regulators, chromatin modifier complexes (such as CRL4B), and their role in breast tumorigenesis and environmental health. Dr. Ma’s scholarly contributions include high-impact peer-reviewed publications in journals such as Cell Death & Differentiation, Advanced Science, and Frontiers of Medicine. In addition to his research achievements, he was recognized with the National Voluntary Blood Donation Contribution Award (Bronze Award, 2020–2021) and consecutive Peking Union Medical College Academic Scholarships.

Dr. Muhammad Wajeeh Us Sima Rathore
Postdoctoral Researcher
Ningbo University

Dr. Muhammad Wajeeh Us Sima is a Computer Science Scholar and AI Researcher specializing in Deep Learning and Medical Image Analysis. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Chongqing University and is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Ningbo University, where his research focuses on the Spatial Mapping of the Tumor Microenvironment in breast cancer. With over 5 years of research experience, Dr. Wajeeh advances AI for complex medical applications, including computational histopathology, stain generalization, and multimodal imaging (MRI, X-ray, and HIP-CT). His interdisciplinary work also encompasses privacy-preserving healthcare AI and intelligent security for Medical IoT (IoMT) systems. Dr. Wajeeh has authored 11+ peer-reviewed journal papers in high-impact venues (Journal of Big Data, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, BioData Mining, Image and Vision Computing, Internet of Things, Frontiers in Medicine), alongside conference papers and a book chapter on retinal microvascular analysis for diabetic retinopathy. A former University Lecturer and an active reviewer for prestigious international journals, he was honored with the Chongqing University Academic Research Innovation Award (2025).

Dr. Muhammad Waqas Nasir
Postdoctoral Researcher
Ningbo University

Dr. Muhammad Waqas Nasir is a Postdoctoral Researcher specializing in biochemistry, molecular biology, structural biology, and antibody engineering. He earned his Ph.D. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). Dr. Nasir’s research centers on structure-guided antibody engineering, viral immunology, and structure-based therapeutic development, with a focus on SARS-CoV-2 neutralizing antibodies and mechanisms of viral immune escape. He combines experimental techniques—including X-ray crystallography, protein engineering, and molecular biology—with computational modeling to examine antibody recognition and engineer potent therapeutics. His structural contributions include the crystal structure of the SARS-CoV-2 receptor-binding domain in complex with the XG83 Fab (PDB ID: 9UTF). Dr. Nasir has published key research articles in top journals such as the European Journal of Medicinal Chemistry, Microbiology Spectrum, Journal of Structural Biology: X, and Journal of Medical Virology. His academic achievements have been recognized with the prestigious CAS-ANSO Scholarship for Young Talents and participation in international AEARU Global Learning Initiatives.

Dr. Unnikrishnan P. S.
Postdoctoral Researcher
Ningbo University

Dr. Unnikrishnan P. S. is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Ningbo University specializing in biotechnology, protein biochemistry, molecular biology, and DNA repair mechanisms. He earned his Ph.D. in Biotechnology from the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH), where his doctoral research investigated the selective inhibition of the human DNA repair enzyme ALKBH2, protein-ligand dynamics, and the development of cell-permeable fluorescent probes for live-cell imaging to overcome chemoresistance in cancer cells. In his current research at Ningbo University, Dr. Unnikrishnan focuses on elucidating the role of WBP2 in the DNA damage response and repair, exploring its involvement in cellular metabolism, and advancing targeted cancer therapeutics. Dr. Unnikrishnan has authored 10 peer-reviewed publications across leading journals including Talanta, Journal of Biological Chemistry, DNA Repair, Biochemical Journal, and Dalton Transactions, along with a book publication. His academic distinctions include qualifying prestigious national competitive examinations such as the CSIR-UGC NET JRF, GATE, and TIFR-JGEEBILS.

Dr. Baitul Islam
Postdoctoral Researcher
Ningbo University

Dr. Baitul Islam is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Basic Medical Sciences, Ningbo University. He earned his Ph.D. in Microbiology from the Xiangya School of Basic Medical Sciences at Central South University, where his doctoral research focused on elucidating the molecular mechanisms of tumorigenesis and identifying potential therapeutic targets for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). Integrating molecular biology, cancer genetics, and bioinformatics, Dr. Islam’s current postdoctoral research focuses on protein trafficking in cancer and drug discovery for breast cancer. Dr. Islam has authored research articles in peer-reviewed international journals including Phytomedicine, Epigenetics, and the Journal of Biomolecular Structure & Dynamics, with recent contributions in drug discovery and prognostic biomarkers for HBV-related HCC. His scholarly work was recognized with the Third Prize for Outstanding Oral Presentation at the International Symposium on Cryobiology and Living Biobanking (CBLB).
