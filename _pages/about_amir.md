**Dr. Amir Sabbaghian** is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Ningbo University specializing in Molecular Medicine and Translational Cancer Research. With a strong background in cross-institutional research—including a prior Postdoctoral Fellowship at Zhejiang University and research roles at Golestan University of Medical Sciences—his work bridges oncology, functional genomics, and biomarker discovery. Dr. Sabbaghian's primary research investigates critical drivers of oncogenesis and therapy response, particularly in HER2-positive breast cancer, non-coding RNA networks, and oncogenic signaling axes such as WBP2 and WFIKKN2. His broader expertise includes *in silico* drug repurposing, pan-cancer transcriptomics, and circulating blood-based biomarker signatures for early detection and disease monitoring. To date, he has authored 9 peer-reviewed publications across top-tier biomedical journals—including *Cells*, *Biomarker Research*, and the *International Journal of Cancer*—and presented at international scientific conferences.
