**Dr. Baitul Islam** is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Basic Medical Sciences, Ningbo University. He earned his Ph.D. in Microbiology from the Xiangya School of Basic Medical Sciences at Central South University, where his doctoral research focused on elucidating the molecular mechanisms of tumorigenesis and identifying potential therapeutic targets for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). Integrating molecular biology, cancer genetics, and bioinformatics, Dr. Islam's current postdoctoral research focuses on protein trafficking in cancer and drug discovery for breast cancer. Dr. Islam has authored research articles in peer-reviewed international journals including *Phytomedicine*, *Epigenetics*, and the *Journal of Biomolecular Structure & Dynamics*, with recent contributions in drug discovery and prognostic biomarkers for HBV-related HCC. His scholarly work was recognized with the Third Prize for Outstanding Oral Presentation at the International Symposium on Cryobiology and Living Biobanking (CBLB).
