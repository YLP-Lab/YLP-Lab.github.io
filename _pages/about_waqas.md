**Dr. Muhammad Waqas Nasir** is a Postdoctoral Researcher specializing in biochemistry, molecular biology, structural biology, and antibody engineering. He earned his Ph.D. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). Dr. Nasir's research centers on structure-guided antibody engineering, viral immunology, and structure-based therapeutic development, with a focus on SARS-CoV-2 neutralizing antibodies and mechanisms of viral immune escape. He combines experimental techniques—including X-ray crystallography, protein engineering, and molecular biology—with computational modeling to examine antibody recognition and engineer potent therapeutics. His structural contributions include the crystal structure of the SARS-CoV-2 receptor-binding domain in complex with the XG83 Fab (PDB ID: 9UTF). Dr. Nasir has published key research articles in top journals such as the *European Journal of Medicinal Chemistry*, *Microbiology Spectrum*, *Journal of Structural Biology: X*, and *Journal of Medical Virology*. His academic achievements have been recognized with the prestigious CAS-ANSO Scholarship for Young Talents and participation in international AEARU Global Learning Initiatives.
